\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study documents a tamper-evident research state machine in one controlled
local campaign. Two parent-linked equation-discovery candidates fail their
unseen criteria and remain visible as negative evidence. A later workflow
matrix exercises parent linkage, pre-outcome freezing, permissioned memory,
failure feedback, deterministic adjudication, exact replay, and claim-evidence
checks.

The corrected unit of analysis is the task, not the deterministic
task-repetition cell. Across ten tasks, the observed full-loop minus
execute-once mean is \TaskLevelGain{}, the task bootstrap interval is
[\TaskLevelCILower{}, \TaskLevelCIUpper{}], and the exact two-sided sign-test
value is \SignTestTwoSided{}. These values replace the earlier 30-cell
interval in publication-facing text and do not establish comparative
performance on a task population.

The defensible contribution is therefore an auditable controlled
demonstration and a clarified evidence boundary. Interoperable open-science
packaging, independently authored and prospectively powered confirmation,
external baselines under common budgets, and independent human publication
review remain necessary. Until those gates close, the object is a research
record and manuscript candidate, not a submission-ready result.
