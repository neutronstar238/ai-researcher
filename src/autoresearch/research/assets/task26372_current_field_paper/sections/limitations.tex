\section{Limitations and Responsible Use}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The workflow matrix is co-designed.}
The controller, injected faults, repair actions, and evaluator were designed
within one project. The matrix tests whether implemented contracts interact as
specified. It does not estimate performance on tasks written by independent
researchers, and its absolute success proportions may be optimistic.

\paragraph{The independent sample contains ten tasks.}
The additive analysis correctly collapses the deterministic repetitions to
\TaskCount{} task outcomes. This avoids pseudoreplication but leaves a small,
heterogeneous sample. The interval is wide, the two-sided sign test does not
cross a conventional 0.05 threshold, and neither analysis was prospectively
powered as an external confirmation.

\paragraph{Route B does not sample stochastic controller behavior.}
The three identifiers test idempotency and accidental identifier dependence.
They do not measure model-sampling variability, trajectory reliability, or
repair diversity. A confirmation should sample independently generated
trajectories within each independently authored task and retain task as the
primary unit.

\paragraph{Task families and comparisons are narrow.}
The matrix contains four tabular-data workflows and six equation-discovery
trace workflows. It excludes laboratory robotics, human participants,
sensitive clinical data, large-scale training, and long-horizon literature
synthesis. No current external research system is executed under the same
compute, tool, and retry budget.

\paragraph{Previously observed traces are behavior evidence only.}
The six MDBench traces were inspected during the earlier scientific campaign.
They cannot support a new method claim. The two Route A panels are unseen for
their respective candidates, and both outcomes are negative.

\paragraph{Source maturity is mixed.}
The field position uses peer-reviewed 2025--2026 articles where available and
labels CORE-Bench, PaperBench, the KOSMOS audit, SciIntegrity-Bench, and AI
Agents That Matter as preprints. The retained snapshots establish what was
checked on 31 July 2026, not permanent completeness of a fast-moving field.

\paragraph{Local hashes are not an open-science profile.}
The package has file hashes, lineage, a claim ledger, and rebuild commands.
It has not completed RO-Crate 1.3, Workflow Run RO-Crate, PROV-O, persistent
identifier, or rights mapping. Metadata interoperability is planned as a
separate additive layer and will not be represented as independent
confirmation.

\paragraph{Human responsibility is unresolved.}
Automated checks cannot decide novelty, scientific importance, ethical
acceptability, author identity, licenses, AI-use disclosure, venue policy, or
public-distribution risk. Those fields remain unresolved or false. The system
must not upload, distribute, or submit the object without explicit human
approval.

\paragraph{Safety scope is local and computational.}
The recorded experiments use local computation and public data, with no cloud
GPU rental, private data, physical actuation, or autonomous communication.
Moving to physical experiments, sensitive records, or public actions requires
new containment, approval, and monitoring controls.
