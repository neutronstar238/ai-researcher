\section{Limitations and Responsible Use}

\paragraph{Controlled transitions are not open-ended discovery.}
The \routeb{} harness applies frozen deterministic repair transitions to
controlled workflow faults. It does not ask a model to invent arbitrary code
repairs or discover a new scientific law. Because the evaluator and repair
policy were designed together, the absolute success rates may overestimate
performance on independently authored tasks. The ablations test internal
mechanism consistency, not broad autonomous-research competence.

\paragraph{Seeds do not provide independent stochastic policies.}
The three seeds produce independently identified and hash-checked cells, but
the current controller transition function is deterministic. Repeated seed
outcomes therefore test idempotency and accidental seed dependence, not
variation from model sampling. The bootstrap interval is over 30 task-seed
pairs and consequently includes repeated outcomes for each task. Treating all
30 as fully independent would be too strong. A future confirmatory study
should bootstrap at the task level or use independently sampled controller
trajectories.

\paragraph{Task coverage is narrow.}
The matrix contains four tabular-data workflows and six equation-discovery
trace workflows. It does not cover laboratory robotics, human participants,
private clinical data, large-scale training, or long-horizon literature
claims. The source evidence is real, but each injected fault is a workflow
construct. Results should not be generalized beyond comparable
file-oriented, deterministic research pipelines.

\paragraph{Revealed traces are behavior evidence only.}
The MDBench traces had already been revealed during \routea{}. They cannot
serve as unseen evidence for a method claim. We label them at the source,
preregistration, report, and claim-evidence layers. The two \routea{} panels
do provide new unseen evaluation for their respective candidates, and both
results are negative. We do not claim a successful new equation-discovery
algorithm.

\paragraph{Local model role is limited.}
The model proposes policy language and structured hypotheses, but the
deterministic harness chooses all transitions and decisions in \routeb{}.
One local request timed out and used the declared fallback. This is useful for
availability testing but does not establish that Qwen or another model can
autonomously generate every successful repair.

\paragraph{Novelty and review remain human responsibilities.}
Automated citation checks verify identifiers and source availability, not
whether every related work interpretation is complete. Hashes detect changed
files, not conceptual errors. The internal contribution gate and paper audit
are engineering controls and must not be represented as peer review or venue
acceptance. Every external upload remains blocked pending human review of
novelty, scientific value, authorship, licensing, privacy, and venue policy.

\paragraph{Safety boundary.}
Experiments execute locally and use no cloud GPU, paid external model, private
dataset, or autonomous publication action. The campaign cannot modify safety,
license, or submission policies through self-iteration. Extending the system
to physical experiments, sensitive data, or public communication would
require new approval and containment mechanisms.
