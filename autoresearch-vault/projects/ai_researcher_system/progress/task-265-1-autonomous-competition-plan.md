# Task 265.1 autonomous formal competition plan

## Why this route exists

The two official MDBench cycles are real negative evidence, but they are not a
research article autonomously originated by the system. The first candidate
and the weak-form recovery candidate were authored in code before execution.
Other autonomy demonstrations either ranked a fixed catalogue or generated one
bounded expression outside the formal MDBench route.

The scientific evidence is also insufficient for a positive competition
claim. The first candidate's failure-aware median improvement was `0.371535`,
but its system-level 95% interval was `[-0.201060, 0.888991]`. The recovery
candidate's value was `-1.704061`, with interval
`[-4.116249, 0.292912]`. Neither cycle passed the confidence gate.

Task `265` therefore starts a new formal route in which the system must perform
literature refresh, multi-family hypothesis generation, exact-code
implementation, objective execution, branch selection, mutation, ablation,
replication, and manuscript generation inside one append-only ledger.

## Frozen autonomous-origin rules

- The human supplies one high-level competition objective before the run.
- The runtime must generate 8 to 12 exact-code candidates across at least three
  mechanism families and two generations.
- Human-authored candidates, fixed candidate catalogues, post-start human
  algorithm choices, code-side scientific repairs, and LLM self-certification
  are forbidden.
- Every branch and failure remains in comparative memory. Only objective
  development execution can drive selection and mutation.
- The manuscript, figures, tables, claims, and limitations must be generated
  from the same run ledger. Legal authorship, licensing, release, and submission
  remain human decisions.
