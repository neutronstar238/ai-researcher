## What the 2025-2026 field changes

- Peer-reviewed AI Scientist, AI co-scientist, Robin, and ERA systems mean that
  end-to-end loop breadth or Agent count is no longer a sufficient novelty
  claim.
- AstaBench and REPRO-Bench separate agent execution from external task
  validity; CORE-Bench, PaperBench, and independent system audits expose
  completion, reproduction, and correctness failures that local demos cannot
  rule out.
- Graph, Harness, Loop, and Open Science are therefore treated as one evidence
  architecture: explicit evidence states and contradictions; independent-unit,
  budget, and execution boundaries; registered stop rules with retained
  failures; and portable provenance.
- The defensible contribution is a failure-linked, tamper-evident research
  state machine. A scientific-effect claim still requires independently
  authored tasks, external baselines, common budgets, an independent scorer,
  and human review.
